BOTF Remastered PC beta (round 9): what changed since 0.1.566

The round 8 notes (Gemini audit fixes) and the 0.1.538 to 0.1.566 notes follow the new ones.

New in this build (round 9)
- Peace deals can hand over worlds. When you are at war, the Diplomacy screen shows a war score (-100 losing to +100 winning). A Peace proposal can name up to three of your border worlds to hand over and up to three of theirs to ask for (never a capital or a last world). The AI offers worlds when it is clearly losing and accepts or refuses a deal by the war score; a winning AI will not give a world up. Proposals the AI sends you name the worlds.
- Invasion odds. The Invade action shows the chance of taking the world (your troops against its population / 5 plus batteries and shields) before you commit. There is still no ground combat; the odds are the same figures the invasion uses.
- Name your flagship. Fleets, Flagship row: type a name and press Rename. It is saved and shows in battle logs.
- Megaprojects have a name per power (for example the Federation Genesis-class Project, the Klingon Praxis Restoration Project).
- Real-time battle and Take the helm are marked "Beta" in their tooltips: they work, and are still being tuned.
- Ship textures unload: the battle keeps the 40 most recently used hulls and forgets the rest, so a long campaign or a long browse of the Encyclopedia does not run the graphics card out of memory.
- Earth and other worlds in the system view show their buildings as small lights on the surface instead of coloured cubes; the battle starbase is toned like the other hulls.
- LAN: each player plans their own ships in a battle and the commanders resolve the round together; a player who leaves is replaced by the AI until they rejoin (they rejoin at the start of a turn).
- Saves: schema 27. Older saves load; a game that never uses the new features saves the same apart from the header.

Fixes in this build (Gemini audit, round 8)
- Battle: the ship brackets, name tags and the radar are placed from the real view size, so they sit on the ships and the radar is in the bottom-right corner while a round plays (they sat up-left of the ships and the radar floated over the starbase).
- New campaign: the Launch campaign button is on a bar under the options, always on screen (1920x1080 and 1366x768).
- Tutorial: "This is the star chart" and the other steps show only on the screen they teach from; they no longer sit over the System, Empire and Diplomacy panels.
- Title: "Eight powers" and all eight emblems; the corner label shows the build number, not "dev build".
- Diplomacy and Empire: the record row and the member-worlds pill have labels.
- LAN: players on the sixth to eighth powers can be seated, and a player can only send orders for their own seat.

--- 0.1.538 to 0.1.566 ---
Rules you can feel in a campaign
- C1-C4 were already in 0.1.538. New since then:
- C5: AI powers only form or renew alliances while a third surviving major is at war with one of them. The "allied to everyone" bloc is gone.
- C6: when only two powers are left and both are AI, they stop making treaties and the stronger one goes to war.
- C7: New campaign has a "Domination victory" setting (on by default). Off means domination never ends the game and conquest is the military win. It shows on the end screen and carries into hotseat and LAN.
- C8: invading a world is easier: its defence now counts population / 5 instead of / 3, so a world holds less against a landing and wars between equals end sooner. (Earlier notes had this backwards.)
- C9: AI-to-AI non-aggression, friendship and affiliation also lapse at their 100-turn review unless two AIs share a common enemy. Your own treaties are untouched.
- C11: a minor world you (or an AI) take by invasion stays conquered; it never secedes. Worlds that joined by treaty can still leave. Older saves load and are sorted into one or the other.
- D1: the debt rule. With a negative treasury you cannot buy anything with credits (rush-buys, gifts, credit offers, flagships, station upgrades, megaprojects). The screen tells you why, every colony's morale drops toward 30 while you are in debt, and you get a warning when debt starts and every 10 turns after. The AI in debt only builds farms and power plants.
- Start credits: the Advanced era starts with 9,500 credits (was 7,000, an idle start ran into debt by turn 37-45), and drills that begin in a deficit get credits for 40 turns of it.

Fixes
- Toasts keep clear of every panel, so a "Loaded ..." message no longer sits over buttons. The end screen clears toasts when it opens.
- A loaded campaign no longer shows the first-run tutorial again.
- Battle open: opening a battle straight after loading a save no longer stalls (about 50 ms, was about 100-135 ms).
- AI: a beaten AI with no worlds stops planning, so leftover ships or minor-world offers no longer hand it worlds back. AI transports and groups stop waiting forever at worlds whose guard they cannot break. The AI no longer proposes a Full Alliance between the last two powers. AI avoids running into debt.
- The AI now brings its first war to the human by turn 60 at the latest.
- Save format is now schema 26; saves from older builds load.
