# BOTF Remastered: Parity Matrix

Compiled by Tuvok (Lane L1), 8 Nov 2026. Authoritative comparison between vanilla *Star Trek: Birth of the Federation* (1999), major community mods (Ultimate Dominion Mod III, Supremacy, Rise of the UFP, Birth of the Empires, Ultimate Editor), our C++ rules core today, our Godot UI client today, and our modernization targets across milestones M1–M4.

### Target Classification Key
- **Faithful:** Replicates exact original BOTF rules, statistics, and mechanics.
- **Corrected:** Fixes documented vanilla engine bugs, exploits, crashes, or math inconsistencies.
- **Modernised:** Replaces outdated 1999 conventions with modern 4X game design (e.g. 3D galaxy map, WEGO combat with shield facings and subsystem targeting, colony governors, automated build templates).

### Workstream Key (MASTER-PLAN §5)
1. Game design & balance | 2. Galaxy & strategic map | 3. Systems, planets & economy | 4. Research | 5. Ships, fleets & logistics | 6. Combat | 7. Diplomacy, intelligence & minors | 8. Majors, eras & threats | 9. AI | 10. Art | 11. Audio & voice | 12. UX, onboarding & platform
