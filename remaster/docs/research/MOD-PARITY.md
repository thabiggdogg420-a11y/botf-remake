## 2. What each pack adds, concretely

D = pure data (our `data/base/*.json` and a pack overlay), M = rules code (C++ core), A = assets.

**P1 UDM III.** Dominion replaces the Ferengi as a playable race (own fleet, structures, philosophy, new voices); new minors Breen, Son'a, Maquis, Ferengi, Miradorn, some with ships and buildable on membership (D); Bajor replaces Edo, DS9 replaces the Edo Guardian, Borg sphere replaces the cube, species 8472 replaces the Husnock (D + spawn actors M); shared empire-wide morale structure (M); Shapeshifter Network sabotage structure and Protectorate HQ for subjugated systems (D + M); armed troop transports and colony ships (D + M); stronger orbital batteries and shield generators, rebalanced populations, revised tech progression, adjusted trade and ground-combat bonuses (D); eight-phaser models, new intro video, sounds (A); larger map options, new start-system options (D); recloak combat command removed, Orion and Maquis bonuses switched to raiding, ship training fix, task force code for big fleets (M); AI buy priorities standardised, AI random events off, costlier minor gifts, every minor has dilithium (D + M); 1024x768 install; Dominion War scenario add-on (M).

**P2 GALM.** Timeline 2240 to 2380 with tech levels 1 / 4 / 7 / 10 / 14 across five stages (M + D); five empires: Dominion Alliance (Cardassian, Breen, Jem'Hadar, Karemma), Federation, Borg, Klingon, Romulan (M + D); Borg are playable ("Playing as Borg in GALM"), priced high and slow, with Borg structures giving +35 morale, "Morale when ruled by" 100 and every minor Enraged under Borg rule (D + M); ships as close to canon as possible; lower costs and upkeep for the others so they can field big fleets; new minors; species 8472 and Borg/Romulan star types under poll (D). Opening post gives no AI or size detail.

**P3 BoP.** Optional larger maps to 52x39; up to 8 extra starting systems at any level; more stars; up to 9 planets per system (M + D); minors advance smoothly with slower tech (M); automatic, faster building upgrades (M); building and ship stats reworked; weapon outputs adjusted for longer battles; new industry and economy balance (D); more ships than vanilla (D); "significant modifications of game mechanics" (unlisted); AI always builds starbases and the newest ships (M); upgrade-and-scrap and insta-terraform exploits fixed (M).

**P4 UM5 v5.5 Redux.** Error-correction patches added and bad old ones removed; new buildings for all empires, especially Dominion, many building bonuses changed (D); shiplist overhaul with a firepower reduction, new hand-built designs (Cardassian destroyers, Klingon heavy destroyer, Federation Nova and Prometheus, reworked Romulan capitals) (D + A); intel adjusted so AI frames the player less, Cardassian and Romulan intel strength cut (D); research costs scaled back to help the AI advance (D); one new minor (D); 346 new Dominion audio clips and race intros (A); AI patches for smoother and more aggressive expansion (M); 1024x768 and 1366x768 GUI options, Borg spawn code and an ini switch to turn Borg off (M); a version with self-upgrading industry structures is referenced by a UDM poster.

**P5 AAM 1.1H.** Ships replaced for every major to cover Enterprise through Nemesis, from forum ship packs (Ferengi excluded) (D + A); each empire keeps its original strengths and weaknesses, toned down, with offsetting abilities (D); empire buildings reorganised, some minors changed, longer tactical combat, slightly altered random events (D); terraformable home planets at the two earliest start levels, Federation intel defences (D).

## 4. Conflicts between packs, and how we offer them

| Conflict | Packs | Proposal |
|---|---|---|
| Dominion replaces the Ferengi (five slots) | UDM replaces; vanilla, AAM, UM5 keep Ferengi | Our core holds a superset of empires; a preset picks the lineup (up to 5 or 6 slots). The Ferengi stay in the base pack. The Dominion War preset swaps Ferengi for Dominion, as UDM did |
| Lineup without Cardassians or Ferengi (Dominion Alliance contains Cardassians) | GALM | Galaxies preset: Federation, Klingon, Romulan, Dominion Alliance (with Cardassian and Breen as sub-lines), Borg. Needs MOD-2 and the Borg playable |
| Tech cap 10 against 14, and era start as tech level | vanilla/UDM/BoP against GALM | Tech levels 11 to 14 exist only under the Galaxies and Ages presets; other presets cap at 10 |
| Edo against Bajor, Husnock against species 8472, cube against sphere | UDM against vanilla | Pack toggles on the entity and minor lists; the Dominion War preset applies UDM's substitutions |
| Longer battles and firepower cuts disagree (BoP longer, UM5 less firepower, UDM eight phasers) | BOP, UM5, UDM | Do not copy numbers. Simon's combat rulings win; each pack becomes a "balance overlay" of costs and stats and combat stays on our one rules set |
| Ship replacement sets (AAM, UM5, UDM, GALM each rebuild the list) | all | One superset roster tagged by era and pack; presets choose which classes are buildable. We already hold 98 expanded classes across 8 eras, which is the AAM idea |
| Slower minor tech (BoP) against UDM's costlier gifts | BOP, UDM | Both are data on the minor roster; presets differ only by overlay |
| Auto-upgrade structures against "No Building Upgrades" mod | BOP/UM5 against NBU | One rule flag `structureAutoUpgrade` |

**Presets, as a pack manifest plus a ruleset flag set:** Classic BOTF (vanilla); Remastered (default: vanilla plus our layers); **Dominion War** (UDM); **Ages** (AAM: era picker, canon ship lists); **Galaxies** (GALM: 2240 to 2380, Borg and Dominion Alliance lineup, tech 14); **Balance of Power**; **Ultimate** (UM5 balance numbers and Dominion buildings). Each preset is a list of data overlays, a lineup and a flag set. The new-campaign screen shows a preset dropdown and the era list beside it.
