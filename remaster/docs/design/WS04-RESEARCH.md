# Workstream 04: Research

## 1. Reference Behaviour (BOTF and Mods)

In vanilla *Birth of the Federation*:
- **Six Fields of Ten Levels:** Research was structured across six distinct academic disciplines: Biotechnology, Energy, Computers, Propulsion, Construction, and Weapons [AFC-CAL, AFC-STR]. Each field progressed through Levels 0 to 10 (totaling 66 named technologies plus baseline level 0 entries, represented by 77 records in modern data tables) [AFC-CAL, docs/research/CURRENT-STATE-AUDIT.md:61].
- **Research Allocation:** Research points were generated by population assigned to the research work area and boosted by lab structures [TREKCORE]. Players allocated funding percentages across the six disciplines, with points accumulating toward calculated tier thresholds [AFC-CAL].
- **Tech Unlocks:** Advancing tech levels unlocked new ship classes, upgraded colony structures (e.g., higher-tier power plants or factories), and improved scanner ranges [TREKCORE, AFC-SHIP]. Ships under construction or in service automatically upgraded once tech requirements were satisfied, incurring an upgrade cost [TREKCORE, AFC-SHIP].
- **Minor Race Multipliers:** Several minor civilizations provided dramatic research bonuses upon affiliation or membership [AFC-MIN]:
  - Empire-wide percentage bonuses: Vulcans (+35% all fields), Trills (+30% all fields), Caldonians (+25% all fields).
  - Specific field double-output bonuses (+100%): Bandi (Construction), Bynars (Computers), Chalnoth (Weapons), Malconians (Propulsion), Sheliak (Biotech), Takarans (Energy).
  - Flat point grants: Pakleds (+100 flat), Tamarians (+300 flat).
- **Racial Research Aptitude:** In vanilla, baseline research speed varied significantly by faction: Federation was fastest, followed by Romulan, Klingon, Cardassian, and Ferengi [AFC-CAL].

Mods and Remakes:
- **Supremacy:** Unified the disparate tech ladders into a single navigable graph with branch prerequisites and era-based progression thresholds [Supremacy].
- **UDM3:** Extended technology requirements to include specialized weaponry, escort hulls, and Borg countermeasures [UDM3].

## 3. Web-Build Consequences

In compliance with `docs/M0-LANES.md` ("Browser target") and `docs/ART-BIBLE.md` §12:
- **Renderer Fallback (Forward+ vs Compatibility/WebGL2):** The Research screen 2D node graph and UI sliders render under Compatibility (WebGL2) using standard canvas items. The embedded 3D model preview turntable runs under OpenGL Compatibility with simplified directional lighting (fill and rim unshadowed) and ACES tonemapping.
- **Budgets:** 77 technology icons are packed into a compact sprite atlas; typography is subset to Latin and Latin-1 woff2 fonts (≤60 KB each). 3D models rendered in the preview sub-viewport use web-budget meshes (LOD0 12k to 45k tris, 2 LOD levels, 1024 textures, ETC2/ASTC compressed) to ensure rapid loading without memory spikes.
- **No Disk Paths at Runtime:** Technology names, costs, prerequisites, and descriptions load from `data/base/technologies.json` handed to the core as a byte buffer (`referenceData::TechnologyCatalogue`, `ReferenceCatalogue.h:11`). Unlocked 3D models and icons load strictly through `res://` packs. Zero runtime disk reads or `$BOTF_*` env vars.
- **Saves:** Research progress points, current allocations, locked slider states, and accumulated overflow reserves serialize to `user://`, which maps to browser IndexedDB in web builds.
