# BOTF beta fidelity notes: planets, terraforming, and intelligence

This note records mechanics that can be checked against the original player tutorial/manual and community reverse-engineering. It separates source-backed behavior from gaps in the local data extraction. “Confirmed” below means that a source explicitly describes or measures the behavior; it does not mean that the All-in-One installer overlay has been proven byte-identical to retail 1.0.2.

The tutorial gives a safe aggregate fixture: Melina has three planets with **235 total population capacity after all three are terraformed**. It also shows the class-letter state transition: a partially terraformed planet is yellow; a habitable/Terran or completely terraformed planet is green; at least one green planet is needed before colonizing the whole system. This is a system-level capacity example, not a planet-type lookup table ([original tutorial, pages 8–9](https://gaming.trekcore.com/birthofthefederation/downloads/tutorial.pdf); the same tutorial is extracted at [`tutor.txt`](reference-local/aio-1.0.1-vanilla/app/Vanilla/tutor.txt#L93)). The [1999 game manual](https://manualmachine.com/gamespc/birthofthefederation/1118235-user-manual/) documents the strategic map/system information and terraforming screens (manual pages 55–56).

The manual/tutorial describe the map hover as the place to inspect the actual planet’s type, maximum population, growth, and habitability, so capacity belongs to each planet record and the sum is a system value. A community play example reports O-class capacity 100, G-class 80, and Y-class 70, but it is not a complete table or a controlled source-data dump; use these only as examples, not constants ([community terraforming examples](https://www.armadafleetcommand.com/onscreen/botf/viewtopic.php?t=1403)). Planet codes/classes reported in the reverse-engineering formula are P, J, G, L, O, M, and Y; a separate modding explanation lists internal type IDs including gas giant, but these source-ID details do not provide capacities ([planet/atmosphere formula](https://www.armadafleetcommand.com/onscreen/botf/viewtopic.php?t=58), [planet-type workaround discussion](https://www.armadafleetcommand.com/onscreen/botf/viewtopic.php?p=44943)).

**Acceptance cases for planet data and presentation**

- Preserve source planet ID, type/class, atmosphere, maximum population, resources, and current terraforming/habitability state as distinct fields once the parser is validated. Unknown fields remain absent/unknown, never populated from class averages.
- Reproduce the Melina tutorial fixture: three planets; aggregate capacity 235 only after all are fully terraformed; partial = yellow; complete/habitable = green; system colonization is gated by at least one green planet. The colony ship is consumed/dismantled by colonization, and colonization applies to the system rather than a single planet ([tutorial, pages 8–9](https://gaming.trekcore.com/birthofthefederation/downloads/tutorial.pdf)).
- For any decoded system, calculate displayed total maximum population from its planet capacities and terraformed state; do not present 235 as a universal system or planet capacity.
- Keep Gas Giant/uninhabitable special-case behavior separate from a general “capacity zero means safe to colonize” shortcut. Modding reports describe hard-coded gas giant checks and historical AI issues; exact behavior should be tested against the chosen ruleset ([community gas-giant discussion](https://www.armadafleetcommand.com/onscreen/botf/viewtopic.php?t=4143)).

## Terraforming work, rate, and technology

The reverse-engineered required work is:

```text
terraform_work = trunc(population_capacity * (planet_type_coefficient + atmosphere_coefficient))
```

Type coefficients: P +8, J +10, G +7, L +6, O +4, M 0, Y +12. Atmosphere coefficients: methane +1.5, none +1, oxygen-rich −1.5, sulfuric +1.25, thin-oxygen −1. A zero/negative result is treated as no terraforming required. An advanced write-up gives the expanded expression `10 * global_multiplier * capacity * (type + atmosphere)` and reports the executable default global multiplier as 0.1, which reduces to the simple formula. Both posts are community reverse-engineering, not a local replay against this installer build ([simple formula and coefficients](https://www.armadafleetcommand.com/onscreen/botf/viewtopic.php?t=58); [advanced multiplier](https://www.armadafleetcommand.com/onscreen/botf/viewtopic.php?t=60)).

Terraforming work is applied per turn by ships in a task force. A reverse-engineering post reports ship production value × 100 as the task-force terraforming capacity: Colony Ship I production 1 gives 100 work/turn; Colony Ship II production 3 gives 300/turn. It also identifies a vanilla bug where troop-transport production can be added to colony-ship output and instant-terraform a planet; that defect and suggested fix must not be mistaken for intended capacity ([task-force terraforming capacity](https://www.armadafleetcommand.com/onscreen/botf/viewtopic.php?t=949)). The original tutorial independently confirms the starting Colony Ship’s 100-per-turn rate and says the number and technology level of colony ships determine how many turns terraforming takes ([tutorial PDF](https://gaming.trekcore.com/birthofthefederation/downloads/tutorial.pdf)).

Biotech research descriptions say the field affects terraforming and farm production; Metagenics describes rapid large-scale terraforming; Climate Control and Dynamic Ecological Modification describe further terraforming applications. This establishes the original design intent, **not an exact direct per-level multiplier** ([local source descriptions](reference-local/validated-catalog/reference.json#L122), [Metagenics](reference-local/validated-catalog/reference.json#L163), [Climate Control](reference-local/validated-catalog/reference.json#L175), [Dynamic Ecological Modification](reference-local/validated-catalog/reference.json#L218)). A community mod patch proposes coupling terraforming output to current Biotech level, which is evidence that this is an optional patch/mod behavior, not proof of the vanilla formula ([advanced-cost discussion and patch reference](https://www.armadafleetcommand.com/onscreen/botf/viewtopic.php?t=60)). Do not invent a universal “Biotech level × terraform work” rule without checking the exact selected executable/patch and ship stats.

**Acceptance cases for terraforming**

- Formula unit test with an explicitly synthetic input: capacity 81, class G (+7), methane (+1.5) gives `trunc(81 × 8.5) = 688` work. Mark the input as synthetic; it validates arithmetic only, not a known planet record.
- A synthetic cost of 250 work should take 3 turns at 100 work/turn and 1 turn at 300 work/turn. More generally, turns are `ceil(remaining_work / task_force_work_per_turn)`; do not tick progress for a task force without a colony ship.
- A planet with an oxygen-rich or thin-oxygen atmosphere and non-positive coefficient sum must require no work. A positive fractional work result truncates at the decimal point, per the reverse-engineering source.
- Colony Ship I and II fixtures contribute 100 and 300 work/turn from their source production values. Transport production must not be silently treated as colony output in a corrected ruleset. If reproducing the original bug for strict parity, isolate it behind an explicit legacy-compatibility test.
- A technology description can be shown as a rule hint, but no direct Biotech throughput multiplier should be asserted unless the selected game data/patch verifies it.
- Test progress across turn/save/load, partial/habitable color/state transitions, multiple ships’ summed work, and completion without overshooting or repeatedly applying completed terraforming.

## Intelligence allocation, internal security, and attack process

The original tutorial describes up to nine allocations: Internal Security plus Espionage and Sabotage for each of four rival empires. They distribute the intelligence budget, and the example is 80% Internal Security plus 5% for each of four foreign activities. Internal Security is adjusted directly and remains fixed while the other allocations are rebalanced; lockable foreign sliders follow the same redistribution model as research. The tutorial says the four department buttons (General, Economic, Science, Military) designate a focus, but does not explain that they are odds rather than direct attack commands ([tutorial pages 29–31](https://gaming.trekcore.com/birthofthefederation/downloads/tutorial.pdf)).

The community reverse-engineering account clarifies the distinction between **attack frequency** and **attack success**:

- Each opponent/area has an area stockpile that increases by that area’s output after bonuses and is cleared after an attack. That stockpile is compared to an attack threshold; below threshold means no attempt, not a failed attempt. The threshold uses the last attack turn, base delay, enemy/difficulty modifier, and treaty label. A zero-percent area decays by 2% of total intelligence output ([attack frequency](https://www.armadafleetcommand.com/onscreen/botf/viewtopic.php?t=2311)).
- Success uses up to the last 20 turns of Espionage + Sabotage output against the target divided by the last 20 turns of the defender’s Security output plus a security-stockpile term, with morale, computer-tech difference, random roll, and difficulty bonuses also involved. The described final categories are success (0–4), failure/nothing (−1), or fiasco (5–9, with possible diplomatic effects). Security is therefore a managed counter-investment, not a binary shield or a percentage chance by itself ([success calculation and outcomes](https://www.armadafleetcommand.com/onscreen/botf/viewtopic.php?t=2311)).
- Department selection is random weighted by four training counters. Buttons change the evolving odds; they do not directly select the next attack’s department or add intel output/stockpile. General raises its own weight and affects the other department weights; exact update behavior is described in the source account. Unknown opponents’ defaults train toward 30 by turn 16 ([department selection](https://www.armadafleetcommand.com/onscreen/botf/viewtopic.php?t=2311)).
- The reverse-engineering source identifies a vanilla allocation-bonus bug: a nonzero slider receives the building bonus as if it were set to 100% (example: 40% allocation plus 50% bonus becomes 90% of total output instead of 60%). The same source documents a bug-fix patch. A faithful implementation must label whether it preserves this bug or adopts the correction; do not accidentally reproduce it by applying bonuses to the total empire output ([bug description and fix](https://www.armadafleetcommand.com/onscreen/botf/viewtopic.php?t=2311)).
- The source author explicitly says the full event table is not deeply analyzed. It gives stolen/destroyed credits as an example reaching 40% of the target total and reports additional AI protection. Do not extrapolate that to exact probabilities/effects for all Economy, Science, and Military operations ([event-analysis limit](https://www.armadafleetcommand.com/onscreen/botf/viewtopic.php?t=2311)).

The technical source is community reverse-engineering and patch discussion, not an official spec. Its `intel.bin` layout and morale formula were checked against the local All-in-One 1.0.1 Vanilla overlay: `research/reference-local/aio-1.0.1-vanilla/app/Vanilla/stbof.res` (SHA-256 `c6af8e1c3f415bd7616ab5baf2b4f30e29a3cbae9b992d6cb59f6ebc56fbfa2b`) contains a root `intel.bin` of 480 bytes (`0x1E0`), SHA-256 `8ecb42960bb132e7d73780103ab662ffa535ddc95b45aa05777e5b65f20a9dbe`. The local resource is an AIO 1.0.1 overlay; byte identity with retail 1.0.2 is not established.

The AFC reverse-engineering account describes five 96-byte (`0x60`) blocks of little-endian DWORDs. Per block: `+0` espionage base attack threshold; `+4` sabotage base threshold; `+8` five enemy-empire threshold multipliers; `+0x1C` five difficulty multipliers; `+0x30` eight `empsInfo` morale-label security multipliers; `+0x50` four treaty threshold multipliers. The local raw vectors, in archive block order and with no inferred species or enum labels, are:

| Block | Base thresholds (`+0`, `+4`) | Enemy multipliers (`+8`, five) | Difficulty multipliers (`+0x1C`, five) | Security morale multipliers (`+0x30`, eight) | Treaty multipliers (`+0x50`, four) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | 240, 500 | 100, 90, 80, 85, 95 | 80, 100, 130, 160, 200 | 70, 78, 92, 96, 100, 110, 105, 115 | 100, 88, 96, 98 |
| 1 | 280, 550 | 110, 100, 90, 95, 105 | 80, 100, 130, 160, 200 | 60, 75, 90, 95, 100, 110, 105, 115 | 100, 90, 101, 101 |
| 2 | 320, 600 | 120, 110, 100, 105, 115 | 80, 100, 130, 160, 200 | 60, 75, 90, 95, 100, 110, 105, 115 | 100, 88, 95, 99 |
| 3 | 360, 650 | 115, 105, 95, 100, 110 | 80, 100, 130, 160, 200 | 60, 75, 90, 95, 100, 110, 105, 115 | 100, 95, 101, 102 |
| 4 | 200, 450 | 105, 95, 85, 90, 100 | 80, 100, 130, 160, 200 | 72, 80, 92, 96, 100, 110, 105, 115 | 100, 85, 95, 95 |

The +`0x30` table confirms that security morale enters as a percentage multiplier on `[(last 20 Security outputs / 20) + (security stockpile * 0.02)]`. The table's eight positions are identified by the source as `empsInfo` morale labels, but their mapping to this project's morale enum or named morale states, and the block-to-empire mapping, have not been verified. Use multiplier `1.0` as a provisional default until those indexes are independently matched. These bytes support the local overlay's raw baseline values only; they do not establish retail 1.0.2 constants. The local cached Ultimate Editor reader inventory has no IntelBin parser, so the layout attribution remains community reverse-engineering rather than a locally validated typed decode. Attack thresholds and event payloads remain ruleset data; do not hard-code interpretations beyond the verified raw values and documented layout.

**Acceptance cases for intelligence**

- Starting tutorial allocation: 80% Internal Security, and 5% each for Cardassian Espionage/Sabotage and Klingon Espionage/Sabotage; total 100%. Editing foreign allocations must leave Security unchanged; directly editing Security must redistribute eligible unlocked foreign shares without exceeding 100%. Include locked-slider cases and zeroed/unknown opponents ([tutorial pages 29–31](https://gaming.trekcore.com/birthofthefederation/downloads/tutorial.pdf)).
- Show Security allocation/output and foreign Espionage/Sabotage allocations separately from stored points and attack readiness. At below-threshold stockpile, the turn has no attack; once threshold is met, attempt resolution can still fail or fiasco.
- Store and load per-area points and the rolling 20-turn output history. After 21 turns, the oldest output must no longer affect the success calculation. Reset the attempted area’s stockpile on attack; exercise zero-slider decay separately from the no-allocation success modifier.
- Run a controlled attack with identical random seed and change only security investment: the attack/defense strength calculation should change while readiness threshold remains independently explainable. Include computer-tech, morale, treaty, and difficulty as explicit inputs so tests can isolate each modifier.
- Department buttons should update preference weights over time; a deterministic seeded draw should select among departments in proportion to the current weights. They must not create output points or directly choose an attack department.
- With a hypothetical 1,000-point empire output, an allocation of 40% and +50% bonus should yield 600 points under proportional bonus semantics. A separate named legacy-parity case may expect the original bug’s 900 points. Make the selected behavior explicit in the ruleset and UI.
- Keep outcomes data-driven. Add operation-specific acceptance cases only where vanilla `intel.bin`, source event tables, or in-game tests establish exact operation effects and ranges; present unknown effects as unknown rather than fabricating a complete espionage tree.
