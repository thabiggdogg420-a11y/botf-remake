# Birth of the Federation tactical combat reference

27 September 2026. Scope: original-inspired command-and-watch tactical combat. This is documentary research, not a runtime parity claim. **VERIFIED SOURCE** means the original manual/tutorial was read and states the behavior; it does not prove the executable follows it in every case. **SECONDARY TECHNICAL** means a community test or disassembly claim, not locally reproduced. **INFERRED** means a remake recommendation. No combat formula is established here.

## Original player-facing contract

| Area | VERIFIED SOURCE: what the original documentation says | Build consequence (INFERRED) |
|---|---|---|
| Entry | On encounter, Hail can avert combat if every party hails; Auto yields results without the battle view; Fight opens tactical combat, where a player may command ships or leave them to AI. Third-party combat over one's colony can be watched without participation. [Manual pp. 62–63](https://pdfcoffee.com/star-trek-birth-of-the-federation-manual-pc-pdf-free.html) | Preserve meaningful manual/auto/spectator routes and a distinct pre-battle choice. |
| Grouping and targeting | Strategic task forces are regrouped into like-class tactical groups. Clicking a friendly ship selects its group; Select Ship narrows to one, Select Group broadens back. A tactic followed by clicking an enemy ship targets its like-class group; a command-class group can target an individual enemy ship. Menus vary by ship capability; starbases have some tactics. [Manual pp. 63–65](https://pdfcoffee.com/star-trek-birth-of-the-federation-manual-pc-pdf-free.html) | Make selection scope and target scope explicit. Keep group orders fast while allowing individual exceptions. “Formation” here is class grouping, not evidence for player-editable geometric formations. |
| Tactics | Assault approaches to medium range; Charge to close; Circle stays at long range; Harry circles close; Flyby passes at medium; Strafe passes close; Evade avoids ships while firing only at enemies that enter range; Ram collides; Retreat withdraws; Hail seeks mutual disengagement. [Manual pp. 64–65](https://pdfcoffee.com/star-trek-birth-of-the-federation-manual-pc-pdf-free.html) | Tactics need visibly different paths, ranges and exposure. Their tooltips should describe intent, while results can vary with situation, ship, tech and experience as the manual warns. |
| Commit and watch | Orders are set between tactical turns; Turn commits all orders, AI then acts, and a round is displayed. The tutorial calls a tactical turn about 15 seconds. Orders persist into the next turn unless changed. A battle may take many rounds; Turn becomes End after resolution. In multiplayer, all players or a timer must finish before resolution. [Tutorial pp. 25–27](https://gaming.trekcore.com/birthofthefederation/downloads/tutorial.pdf), [manual pp. 7–8, 66](https://pdfcoffee.com/star-trek-birth-of-the-federation-manual-pc-pdf-free.html) | Separate planning, committed simulation and playback. Show current standing orders; never require frantic live clicking during the action. The 15-second statement is an approximate fiction/tempo target, not a verified simulation time step. |
| Cloak | A group cloaked before tactical combat automatically uncloaks when assigned a tactic. [Manual p. 64](https://pdfcoffee.com/star-trek-birth-of-the-federation-manual-pc-pdf-free.html) | Show cloak state and decloak feedback at the order boundary. Opening advantage, detection and recloak rules remain unverified. |
| Retreat and conclusion | The tutorial orders noncombatants to retreat: they exit the fight yet stay in the sector while friendlies fight, and remain there if the player wins. The manual says all retreat, mutual hail or a 50-turn draw can end combat. [Tutorial p. 26](https://gaming.trekcore.com/birthofthefederation/downloads/tutorial.pdf), [manual p. 74](https://pdfcoffee.com/star-trek-birth-of-the-federation-manual-pc-pdf-free.html) | Keep tactical exit separate from strategic location and battle outcome. Test winning and losing after partial retreat; do not assume retreat teleports a ship to safety. |
| Weapons, defense and information | The tutorial distinguishes armed Fast Attack ships using close Charge, Command ships using longer-range Assault, and noncombatants. Hover statistics include hull, shields, beam and torpedo condition, damage control and experience. A friendly scout exposes the corresponding enemy statistics. [Tutorial p. 26](https://gaming.trekcore.com/birthofthefederation/downloads/tutorial.pdf), [manual p. 69](https://pdfcoffee.com/star-trek-birth-of-the-federation-manual-pc-pdf-free.html) | Give beams and torpedoes distinct range/accuracy behavior only after targeted validation; show subsystem state and scouting information limits. |
| Camera and review | The original provides rotation, zoom, reset, overhead/chase/front/from-target/toward-target views, ship cycling, a 3D mini-radar, ship status overlay with red hull and green shield bars, an event log, and replay with turn stepping. [Manual pp. 67–74](https://pdfcoffee.com/star-trek-birth-of-the-federation-manual-pc-pdf-free.html) | Combat must be readable at fleet scale and inspectable after each committed round. Replay/event evidence is part of the original loop, not decoration. |

The tutorial gives an exact acceptance scenario: order the transport group to Retreat, destroyers to Charge an enemy group, cruisers to Assault an enemy group, commit Turn, inspect damage, then commit again with persistent orders. [Tutorial pp. 25–27](https://gaming.trekcore.com/birthofthefederation/downloads/tutorial.pdf). Voice confirmation in the tutorial signals that a target order was accepted; the remake needs an equally unambiguous confirmation, with audio optional.

## Secondary technical evidence and cautions

- **SECONDARY TECHNICAL:** Community tests report shield recharge as a fixed `shiplist.sst` field unaffected by crew experience; a higher Defense stat reduces hit probability. Early claims that Damage Control repairs hull during combat were corrected by later event-log experiments indicating reduced *per-hit hull damage*, with a lethal blow not reduced. [Damage Control discussion](https://www.armadafleetcommand.com/onscreen/botf/viewtopic.php?t=268). This is a reason to separate shield recharge, avoidance and hull mitigation; it is not a validated numerical model.
- **SECONDARY TECHNICAL:** A later executable analysis describes weapon base damage, shield level and beam penetration interactions, and notes vanilla code inconsistencies in precalculated damage. Its patch design is explicitly a mod. [Weapon efficiency analysis](https://www.armadafleetcommand.com/onscreen/botf/viewtopic.php?t=4075). Do not import the patch's formula or balance as baseline original behavior.
- **SECONDARY TECHNICAL:** A cloak/stealth analysis distinguishes strategic map cloak from tactical cloak, reports tactical cloak interface bugs, and proposes executable patches. [Stealth and Cloak](https://www.armadafleetcommand.com/onscreen/botf/viewtopic.php?t=66). Proposed fixes and claims about unverified stealth fields must not become vanilla specifications.
- **SECONDARY PLAYER REPORT:** A formation thread reports vulnerable science ships spawning in front and retreating too slowly to survive. [Battle Fleet Formation](https://www.armadafleetcommand.com/onscreen/botf/viewtopic.php?t=2958). This supports a usability test for initial placement and retreat vulnerability; it does not establish placement rules.
- **SECONDARY PLAYER REPORT:** The [Combat mechanics discussion](https://www.armadafleetcommand.com/onscreen/botf/viewtopic.php?t=3080) says visible weapon totals alone poorly predict outcomes and contains tests of firing arcs. It reinforces that a simple sum of displayed weapon damage is inadequate, without providing a complete formula.

## Implementation order and parity gates

1. **P0 — command loop:** entry choice, class groups, group/ship scope, legal capability menus, target selection and confirmation, standing orders, Turn commit, watch, event log and replay. Pass the tutorial's transport/destroyer/cruiser sequence with readable feedback.
2. **P0 — consequences:** distinct movement for the documented tactics, separate shield/hull state, weapon effects, ship destruction, partial retreat, victory/loss/hail/draw and return to strategic state. Use named provisional combat policies where the original formula is unknown; do not claim numeric parity.
3. **P1 — tactical information and spectacle:** scout-gated enemy details, subsystem status, camera presets, zoom, ship cycling, radar, battle status overlay, synchronized sounds and logs. Ensure target and damage are identifiable during playback.
4. **P1 — reference comparison:** capture a controlled fight from the pinned Vanilla install, including class grouping, order availability, opening cloak, first-round trajectories, shield recharge, retreat outcome and replay. Record executable/resource hashes and video/event logs. Compare documented behavior first, then tune numerical behavior from repeatable experiments.
5. **P2 — deeper fidelity:** firing arcs, accuracy, experience, damage control, cloak detection and exact phase ordering. These require isolated tests or trustworthy code tracing; do not fabricate constants from the manual's qualitative descriptions.

The local [BOTF research dossier](BOTF-RESEARCH.md) identifies an extracted All-in-One Vanilla reference, normalized ship records and an Ultimate Editor format reference. Those data can supply ship fields, but their mere presence does not prove combat semantics or runtime behavior. Production combat should expose its provisional rules and tests separately from verified original behavior.

## Independent review clarifications

The original sources conflict on Assault range: the manual (p.64) says medium range, while Tutorial #4 (p.26) describes the cruisers' Group Assault at long range. Preserve this discrepancy until original-runtime comparison; neither text establishes a numerical range formula.

The manual (p.73) also describes a separate in-combat Auto control that delegates tactical orders through completion, togglable before committing Turn. This is distinct from encounter-window Auto, and should be included in command-loop acceptance.
